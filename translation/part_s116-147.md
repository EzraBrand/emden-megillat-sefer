clothe many wondrous aggadot, very strange at first glance, in adorned garments and ornaments more precious than fine gold. "I will sing to the

leader also, an outstanding and dear young man, studied with me, together with other young men, sons of the rich of the community; and I would

agree to allow it — that such a foolish lad should discharge the obligation for them on the holy day. So the hatred between us grew great again,
and he thought to take revenge, and would not pay me my salary, and I was as one who keeps silent.

After this the said old man did a foolish thing, and nearly put himself in danger with the authorities. The matter was that he ordered a
proclamation to be announced in the synagogue which was displeasing to the authorities, for he relied on his power and thought that no one would
dare to approach and reveal the thing to the authorities. But the enemy he had in the community lay in wait for him; he did not sleep and did not
keep silent, but went at once and revealed the matter of the proclamation to the authorities. All the men of the community were summoned to come
before the authorities, so that each one by himself should tell what he knew and had heard of this proclamation. I too was summoned to come to
the authorities, to tell what I had heard.

Now my name was great with them and my reputation very good, especially because of my good and upright conduct in money matters: not only was I
not greedy after it like others, nor "hasty after wealth" (Prov. 28:22), but I was generous in giving up what was mine. And whenever a gentile
⟨S: "an uncircumcised man"⟩ had a lawsuit with a Jew before their courts, they would call me to sit with them in judgment, and they would pay my
fee. Once I made a compromise for a Jew who was sued by a gentile ⟨S: "an uncircumcised man"⟩ before them, and I said that if he would not be
appeased, I would give for him what I had ruled against him, to free him from an oath. That man refused to keep my compromise, so I kept my word
and paid for him as I had said, and became surety for him. In short, this and the like, many, many times. The one who brought the words to the
ears of the authorities made me a name like the name of the great, among Jews and gentiles ⟨S: "Jews and uncircumcised"⟩.
